# 出图说明：地图与场景图

出好的图放进 `assets/`，文件名照下面的写，png 或 webp 都行，我来压缩和接入。

| 文件名 | 用途 | 比例 | 建议尺寸 |
|---|---|---|---|
| map-harbor | 地盘页底图 | 1:1 | 1024×1024（工具支持就出 2048） |
| scene-hall | 祖堂，用于序章、主菜单、内部事件 | 3:2 横 | 1536×1024 |
| scene-street | 雨夜老街，用于月报、街面事件 | 3:2 横 | 1536×1024 |
| scene-pier | 码头夜景，用于血拼战报、外部事件 | 3:2 横 | 1536×1024 |

共同要求：画面里不能有任何文字、招牌字、数字、水印。场景图不要出现能看清脸的人，最多远处背影，免得跟立绘撞人。

## 1. map-harbor

A hand-drawn antique map of a fictional southern Chinese harbor city, seen straight from above, drawn in ink and soft watercolor on aged, yellowed paper. The geography loosely follows Hong Kong: a large mainland mass across the top two-thirds with hilly interior; a peninsula pointing south at the bottom centre of the mainland; a narrow harbor channel separating it from a wide island to the south; a large separate island in the lower left; a smaller peninsula reaching east; a few scattered small islets. Coastlines outlined in fine dark-brown ink, sea washed in pale muted indigo-grey watercolor with faint hand-drawn ripple lines, land left as bare paper tone with light ink hatching for hills and ridges. Subtle fold creases, a coffee ring stain near one corner, slightly worn edges. Calm, sparse, lots of empty paper on the land so markers can be placed on top later. Absolutely no text, no place names, no labels, no numbers, no compass letters, no grid, no roads, no buildings, no legend, no border frame text. Muted palette: paper beige, ink brown, faded indigo. Flat top-down view, no perspective, no 3D.

## 2. scene-hall

Interior of an old clan ancestral hall in a 1980s southern Chinese harbor city at night, after rain. A heavy dark wooden table in the centre with a closed blue cloth-bound account book, a ring of old keys and a carved stone seal on it; empty wooden chairs around it. Red lacquered ancestral altar shelf in the background with dim incense smoke, faded red paper scrolls with no readable characters, an old ceiling fan, iron window grille with rain streaks and distant neon glow outside. Warm low tungsten light against cold blue night from the window. Cinematic photograph, 35mm film, fine grain, shallow depth of field, slightly faded colors like an old print. No people, no readable text anywhere.

## 3. scene-street

A narrow old street in a 1980s southern Chinese harbor city on a rainy night. Wet asphalt reflecting light, tong lau buildings with iron balconies, laundry poles, rolling shutters half down, a small tea house with fogged windows and warm light inside, neon signs glowing red and green but blurred and out of focus so no characters can be read. Steam from a street food stall, puddles, a lone distant figure with an umbrella seen from behind. Cinematic photograph, 35mm film, fine grain, moody and quiet, slightly faded colors like an old print. No readable text, no close-up faces.

## 4. scene-pier

A cargo pier in a southern Chinese harbor at night with light rain. Stacked wooden crates and old shipping containers, a rusting gantry crane, moored fishing boats and a small freighter, sodium-vapor lamps casting orange pools on the wet concrete, dark water with reflections, city lights blurred across the harbor in the distance. Tense, empty, as if something just happened or is about to. Cinematic photograph, 35mm film, fine grain, slightly faded colors like an old print. No people in the foreground, no readable text on crates or boats.
